Give this page and the right file to any printer, embroiderer, sign maker or uniform supplier.

**Which crest:** "GREENFIELD SCHOOL" is the whole school's crest (signs, letterheads, the website, creche and
primary uniforms). "GREENFIELD COLLEGE" is for the secondary section only (secondary uniforms, certificates and
report cards). The shield on its own has no words, so it is the same for everyone.

**Merch crest** is the same crest simplified for small sizes: the motto words are left off (the green band stays),
the name and "Knowledge is Light" are bigger, the sun has 7 bold rays instead of 11, and the edges are heavier. Use it
whenever the crest will be smaller than 75mm on fabric; above that, use the full crest.

**Fonts:** the words use **Fraunces Bold** (GREENFIELD SCHOOL / COLLEGE) and **Plus Jakarta Sans** (motto, ribbon), both free
from Google Fonts. Ask the printer to install them or to **convert text to outlines** before production; otherwise
their software will swap in a different font.

## Colours

The crest has three colours: green, gold and white. Ask for a printed proof or a thread sample before a large order;
screen colours always look brighter than ink or thread.

| Colour | Screen (hex) | Nearest Pantone (confirm with a swatch) | Print (CMYK, approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| School green | `#0B3D2E` | 3435 C | C 88 M 35 Y 78 K 62 |
| Gold | `#C9961A` | 7555 C | C 18 M 40 Y 100 K 5 |
| Gold on dark fabric | `#D4A72C` | 7555 C (or a metallic gold thread) | C 15 M 33 Y 95 K 2 |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | — | paper / white thread |

- **Embroidery:** 3 thread colours (green, gold, white). On dark green fabric use the dark-fabric file: no green
  thread needed except where the fabric is not green.
- **Screen print:** the colour crest is 3 spot colours; the one-colour file is 1. Never print the colour crest as
  CMYK on fabric; the gold goes muddy.

## Smallest sizes

| Version | Print | Embroidery |
|---|---|---|
| Full crest (with motto) | 40mm wide | 75mm wide (blazer pocket, polo chest: 75–90mm) |
| Merch crest | 30mm wide | 60mm wide (polo chest, cap front: 60–75mm) |
| Shield only | 10mm wide | 20mm wide (cap front: 50–60mm) |

Below these sizes the words and the gold lines can't be printed or stitched cleanly. Go down a step: full crest →
merch crest → shield only.

## Space and placement

- Keep clear space around the crest of at least **one-eighth of its width** on every side: no text, seams, zips or
  pocket edges inside it.
- Polo or blazer: left chest, centred 18–20cm below the shoulder seam.
- Cap: merch crest at 55–60mm, or the shield only, centred on the front panel.

## Don'ts

- Don't stretch or squash it (always scale both ways together).
- Don't recolour it, add outlines, shadows, glows or gradients.
- Don't put the colour crest on a busy photo or a mid-tone fabric (use the one-colour or dark-fabric file).
- Don't retype the words or redraw the tree; always use the files above.
- Don't crop off the ribbon or stars of the full crest; use the shield-only file instead.
