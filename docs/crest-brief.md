# Greenfield crest: design brief

Written Nov 2026, from the school's own description, to guide a new crest. The school has the final say on any design.

## The school's story (in the school's words, lightly edited)

Greenfield was founded to help people from any background excel through education. It was born from a love of
educating people and of giving back to society, to leave a lasting impression that improves the value of individual
lives. It sets out to give people good character that will help them in future, so they can be the best wherever they
find themselves, both academically and morally.

## What the crest must say

| The school's idea | What it can become in a picture |
|---|---|
| People from **any background** can excel | An open field: "greenfield" means open, unbuilt land, full of potential, where anything can grow. Many roots joining into one tree. |
| **Love** of educating; **giving back** | Something offered, held up or passed on. Not a heart split in two (reads as a broken heart). |
| **Character and academics**, both | Two equal parts of one thing (two leaves of one seedling; roots and crown of one tree). |
| **Excel**, be **the best anywhere** | A star; something rising. |
| A **lasting** impression | An oak; a seal. Simple shapes that age well. |

## Requirements

- One picture, understood in one sentence. One motto on the crest. The school's name as words beside it, or round a
  seal ring, not crammed into the shield.
- School green `#0B3D2E`, gold `#C9961A`, white. Flat colours only, embroiders at 60mm, works in one colour, reads
  as an icon at 16px.
- Must not read as a religious symbol (a crescent and star, a cross) or look like another Lagos school's crest or a
  known brand. Book-and-sun and torch crests are very common
  among Nigerian schools; avoid them as the main idea.
- Two versions of the same symbol: a shield (blazers, signs, website) and a round seal (certificates, report cards,
  stamps).

## Motto

**Decided (Nov 2026): "Knowledge is Light"** is the crest's motto, on the ribbon and round the seal. It is short enough
to stitch and read small. "Excellence in Character & Academics" stays the school's strapline, for letterheads, the
website and prospectuses, not on the crest.
